# Continuous Monitoring and Review

## Purpose

This document defines how third-party risks are monitored and reviewed throughout the vendor lifecycle.

The objective is to ensure that changes in vendor posture, business usage, or external threat conditions are identified and addressed in a timely manner.

---

## Continuous Monitoring Activities

Ongoing monitoring may include:

- Periodic security reassessments
- Review of updated vendor attestations and certifications
- Monitoring of security advisories and breach notifications
- Tracking changes in vendor services or data handling
- Review of contractual and compliance obligations

Monitoring intensity is proportional to vendor risk tier.

---

## Reassessment Triggers

Formal reassessment may be triggered by:

- Significant changes in vendor services or scope
- Security incidents or reported breaches
- Regulatory or legal changes
- Changes in data sensitivity or access levels
- Expiration of certifications or audit reports

Triggers are documented and acted upon promptly.

---

## Review Cadence

Recommended reassessment frequency:

- High-risk vendors: Annually or upon material change
- Medium-risk vendors: Every 18–24 months
- Low-risk vendors: Every 36 months or as needed

The cadence balances risk exposure with operational efficiency.

---

## Issue Tracking and Escalation

Identified issues are:

- Logged and tracked to resolution
- Assigned clear ownership
- Escalated based on severity and impact
- Reviewed during risk or governance meetings

Unresolved high-risk issues may result in reclassification or escalation to leadership.

---

## Governance and Reporting

Monitoring outcomes are reported to:

- Security and risk management teams
- Procurement and vendor management
- Senior leadership or risk committees (as applicable)

Reports focus on risk trends, exceptions, and decision points rather than technical detail.

---

## Documentation and Audit Readiness

All monitoring activities and decisions are documented to support:

- Internal audits
- Regulatory inquiries
- Third-party reviews
- Evidence-based risk decisions

Documentation ensures transparency and defensibility.

---

## Key Takeaway

Third-party risk management is a continuous governance discipline — not a one-time assessment — requiring structured monitoring, review, and accountability over time.

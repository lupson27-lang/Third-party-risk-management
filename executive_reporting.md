# Executive Reporting

## Purpose

This document shows how vendor risk information is translated into reports for leadership and audit purposes. The goal is to provide **decision-ready insights**, not operational detail.

---

## Reporting Objectives

- Summarize vendor risk across the portfolio
- Highlight high-risk vendors and key issues
- Show trends and changes over time
- Support risk acceptance decisions and audit evidence

---

## Suggested Metrics

- Number of vendors by risk tier
- Number of high-risk findings open vs. mitigated
- Percentage of vendors reassessed on schedule
- Average time to remediate high-risk findings
- Key gaps impacting critical business or regulatory areas

---

## Reporting Format

- Executive summaries with charts/tables
- Risk heatmaps or dashboards (conceptual, not real data)
- Recommendations and decisions required
- Periodic review frequency (quarterly, semi-annual, or triggered)

---

## Governance Notes

- Reports reviewed by security, risk, and business owners  
- Documented for audit traceability  
- Focus on **decision-support**, not operational minutiae

---

## Key Takeaway

Executive reporting bridges the gap between technical assessment and strategic decision-making, ensuring leadership sees **what matters most** in vendor risk.

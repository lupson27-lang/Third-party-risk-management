# Risk Treatment and Acceptance

## Purpose

This document outlines how third-party risks are addressed, mitigated, or accepted following assessment and due diligence activities.

The goal is to ensure that vendor-related risks are handled through structured, documented, and defensible decisions aligned with organizational risk appetite.

---

## Risk Treatment Options

Identified third-party risks may be handled using one or more of the following approaches:

- Risk Mitigation
- Risk Acceptance
- Risk Transfer
- Risk Avoidance

The selected approach depends on risk severity, business impact, regulatory requirements, and operational constraints.

---

## Risk Mitigation

Mitigation actions may include:

- Requiring vendor remediation plans
- Implementing compensating internal controls
- Contractual security requirements
- Increased monitoring or reporting obligations
- Limiting vendor access or data exposure

Mitigation efforts are tracked and reviewed to assess effectiveness.

---

## Risk Acceptance

Risk acceptance may be appropriate when:

- The residual risk falls within defined risk appetite
- Mitigation is not technically or economically feasible
- Business criticality outweighs residual risk
- Compensating controls reduce impact sufficiently

Accepted risks must be formally documented and approved by appropriate stakeholders.

---

## Risk Transfer

Risk transfer mechanisms may include:

- Contractual liability clauses
- Cyber insurance requirements
- Indemnification agreements
- Shared responsibility models

Risk transfer does not eliminate risk but reallocates potential impact.

---

## Risk Avoidance

In some cases, risks cannot be accepted or mitigated to an acceptable level.

Risk avoidance may result in:

- Vendor rejection
- Termination of engagement
- Scope reduction
- Selection of alternative providers

These decisions are documented with supporting rationale.

---

## Approval and Governance

Risk treatment decisions require approval based on risk level:

- Low risk: Security or procurement approval
- Medium risk: Risk management or security leadership
- High risk: Executive or risk committee approval

All approvals are recorded for audit and governance purposes.

---

## Review and Reassessment

Accepted and mitigated risks are periodically reviewed to ensure:

- Risk assumptions remain valid
- Vendor controls have not degraded
- Business usage has not changed
- Regulatory requirements are still met

Reassessment frequency is aligned with vendor risk tier.

---

## Key Takeaway

Third-party risk management is not about eliminating all risk — it is about making transparent, accountable decisions that balance security, compliance, and business needs.
